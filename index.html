How it works

  • Tap the name above the chart to change what the band plays: one chord, a ii–V–I, a blues, rhythm changes, or changes you type yourself, in any key.
  • Drone holds the chord with no time. Comp is a swinging trio. Blocks is Barry Harris: block chords that walk up and down the chord's sixth-diminished scale over bass and drums.
  • The line above the chord is the key whose notes fit it. D−7, G7 and B−7♭5 are all C major's notes. The notes are spelled out under the chord, with the chord's own notes brighter.
  • In Blocks, that line names the two chords taking turns and the row under it is their eight-note scale. The sixth chord's notes are the bright ones: land on those on the beat and the diminished notes take care of themselves.
  • Anything you change while the band is playing comes in on the next bar line, so you can switch feel, key or changes without stopping.
  • Tap the tempo in time to set it, or hold − or + to run it up and down.
  • Every note name here is concert pitch, the same as your tuner.
